01

The problemTrade fairs without return.

Trade · Wed–ThuMeetings that convert.

Buyers already in Berlin for Bar Convent — plus proof of demand to show them.

02

The problemPull you can't prove.

Taste · Thu–FriDemand you can show.

Distributors watch the public taste, react and buy your bottles — live, in the room.

The problemA public drinking less.

Open nights · Thu–FriA new public for quality.

Culture and nightlife bring people back to quality spirits. Taste less, taste better.

Problem 1 reflects what producers tell us. Problem 3: in Germany only 25% of 18–25-year-olds drink alcohol weekly, a historic low (BIÖG, Drogenaffinitätsstudie 2025).
